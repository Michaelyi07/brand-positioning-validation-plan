# Brand Positioning Validation — Interview Guide (Semi-Structured)

**Purpose:** validate the positioning of the member space platform as an "industry resource connection platform" (vs. its current perception as a "venue operator"), through semi-structured interviews with 8–12 screened target members. Design-only document; interviews are not conducted as part of this plan. All names, statements and examples are fictional.

## 0. Consent block (read aloud at the start of every interview, ~1 minute)

> "Thank you for making time. I am researching how member companies find partners and resources, and how they read our positioning. Nothing is being sold today, and there are no right or wrong answers — I am interested in your actual experience. **With your permission, I would like to record audio and keep written notes during our conversation**, so I can quote you accurately. Your answers will be anonymized (coded, no company or personal names), used only for this positioning study, and will not be shared externally without your separate approval. May I record and take notes?"

- If the interviewee declines recording, take written notes only; set `consent_status = notes_only` and `consent_form_id` accordingly in the results template.
- If the interviewee declines both recording and note-taking, stop and thank them; do not include them in the sample.

## 1. Structure

18 questions in three tagged blocks. Every question carries one **primary tag**; questions that also serve a second theme carry a cross-reference (marked `xref`) so that block counts sum to the unique total (6 + 6 + 6 = 18; no question is counted twice).

| Block | Tag | Questions | Theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Q-ND (needs) | Q01–Q06 | Needs and desired outcomes |
| 2 | Q-AL (alternatives) | Q07–Q12 | Current alternatives and workarounds |
| 3 | Q-VP (value proposition feedback) | Q13–Q18 | Reaction to the three value propositions |

Interviewing rules (per The Mom Test): in Blocks 1–2, ask only about the interviewee's past, concrete behavior — never about hypothetical futures or opinions about our idea. Talk less than 20% of the time. In Block 3, value propositions are shown for the first time; collect reaction and comparison data only — do not pitch, defend, or ask "would you use this".

**Administration order in Block 3 (binding):** Q13 (comprehension, one statement at a time) → the paired-comparison ballots and the Q16 ranking task (structured exercise, see `preference_test_plan.md` §2) → Q14 → Q15 → Q17 → Q18. The question numbers define the question set, not the talking order; follow the order given here.

## 2. Block 1 — Needs (Q-ND)

**Q01.** "Think back to the last time you needed to find a business partner, supplier, or resource for your company. Walk me through that episode — what was going on, and what did you do first?"
- Primary tag: needs.
- Purpose: anchor needs in one concrete recent episode, not generalities.
- Flow: opening question; sets the timeline used by Q02–Q05.
- Probe: "What happened next?" / "When exactly was this?" / "Who was involved?"

**Q02.** "Within that episode, what was the hardest part?"
- Primary tag: needs.
- Purpose: locate the pain point the platform would need to own.
- Flow: follows Q01 directly, same episode.
- Probe: "Why was that hard?" / "What had you expected instead?" / "How long did that step take?"

**Q03.** "What was at stake for the company if that hadn't worked out?"
- Primary tag: needs.
- Purpose: gauge severity and priority of the need (functional vs. strategic).
- Flow: deepens Q02 on the same episode.
- Probe: "What did that cost you in time or money?" / "How urgent was it, on a scale of routine to critical?"

**Q04.** "How often does a situation like this come up — and when was the most recent one before this?"
- Primary tag: needs.
- Purpose: estimate frequency; separate one-off episodes from recurring needs.
- Flow: widens from the single episode (Q01–Q03) to a pattern.
- Probe: "Is it seasonal or tied to certain projects?" / "What triggered it each time?"

**Q05.** "In your day-to-day work, what does being a member of this platform actually do for you today?"
- Primary tag: needs. xref: alternatives (Q07).
- Purpose: capture perceived current value in the member's own words (baseline for the shift from venue operator to resource platform).
- Flow: transitions from past episodes to the membership relationship.
- Probe: "Can you give a recent example?" / "What would you say is the single most useful thing?"

**Q06.** "Tell me about the last time the membership clearly helped you. If nothing comes to mind, tell me which member services you have actually used so far — even once."
- Primary tag: needs.
- Purpose: test whether the platform is already associated with resource connection, or only with physical venue services, using only reported past usage.
- Flow: closes the needs block; sets up the alternatives block.
- Probe: "What exactly did you get?" / "How did you use it afterwards?" / "Which services have you touched at all — events, introductions, the portal?"

## 3. Block 2 — Alternatives (Q-AL)

**Q07.** "When you last looked for a partner or resource, who or what did you turn to first — before anything else?"
- Primary tag: alternatives. xref: needs (Q01).
- Purpose: identify the incumbent alternative the platform must beat (peers, agents, trade fairs, search, paid services).
- Flow: parallels Q01 with the alternative lens.
- Probe: "Why that one first?" / "How well did it work?"

**Q08.** "What tools, services or people do you regularly rely on for finding business opportunities? Which of them do you pay for, and how much?"
- Primary tag: alternatives.
- Purpose: map the competitive set, including paid spend (strongest evidence of a real need).
- Flow: generalizes Q07 from one episode to a standing toolkit.
- Probe: "What do you get for that spend?" / "How did you choose it?"

**Q09.** "What have you tried for this and then given up on? What made you stop — and what did you do instead afterwards?"
- Primary tag: alternatives.
- Purpose: surface failed alternatives and switching barriers.
- Flow: follows Q08; same toolkit inventory.
- Probe: "What was the moment you decided to quit?" / "What did you switch to?"

**Q10.** "Who else in your company deals with finding partners or opportunities, and how do they go about it?"
- Primary tag: alternatives.
- Purpose: identify roles and whether the platform must serve non-owner users.
- Flow: extends the alternatives picture across the company.
- Probe: "How do you two divide this work?" / "What do they use that you don't?"

**Q11.** "Think about the past six months: what is one thing you actually got from the platform that you would genuinely miss — and where else can you get that today?"
- Primary tag: alternatives. xref: needs (Q05).
- Purpose: reveal the platform's real, observed substitutability versus the member's current alternatives, grounded in actual recent usage.
- Flow: forces a concrete trade-off comparison with named alternatives.
- Probe: "When did you last actually get that from us?" / "Have you found the same thing elsewhere?" / "How does it compare?"

**Q12.** "The last time a peer company asked you how to find partners or resources in this industry, what exactly did you tell them?"
- Primary tag: alternatives.
- Purpose: collect the member's revealed playbook — the de-facto alternative set, in their own words.
- Flow: closes the alternatives block; transitions into showing the value propositions.
- Probe: "Who asked, and when?" / "Did you mention our platform? In what role?" / "Anything you told them to avoid?"

## 4. Block 3 — Value proposition feedback (Q-VP)

Show the interviewee the three fictional value proposition statements (VP-A, VP-B, VP-C from `preference_test_plan.md`), printed on separate cards, only from Q13 onward. Do not explain or promote them; if asked "which one is you?", answer neutrally: "these are draft directions we are testing." Follow the **administration order** given in §1 (Q13 → ballots + ranking → Q14 → Q15 → Q17 → Q18).

**Q13.** "Here are three statements. Starting with the first one: in your own words, what is it saying?"
- Primary tag: value proposition feedback.
- Purpose: comprehension check — a statement members cannot paraphrase cannot carry the positioning.
- Flow: first contact with the statements; one statement at a time, all three.
- Probe: "What kind of company is that written for?" / "Anything unclear in the wording?"

**Q14.** "Which of the three statements feels closest to the reason you joined — or would keep you as a member? Why that one?"
- Primary tag: value proposition feedback. xref: needs (Q05).
- Purpose: link each statement to membership motivation (needs data from Block 1); administered after the ranking task.
- Flow: after the ballots and Q16 ranking are done.
- Probe: "What in the statement gives you that impression?" / "Which words matter most?"

**Q15.** "Which statement is the least relevant to your company, and why?"
- Primary tag: value proposition feedback.
- Purpose: identify the weakest positioning direction and the reason it fails; counter-balances Q14.
- Flow: after Q14.
- Probe: "What would make it relevant?" / "Is it wrong, or just not for you?"

**Q16.** "Now rank the three statements from most to least relevant to your goals for the coming year. Then tell me briefly why your first choice is first."
- Primary tag: value proposition feedback.
- Purpose: ordinal top-rank data used by the preference test decision rule (`rank` field).
- Flow: administered as a structured exercise immediately after Q13 and the three pair choices (see `preference_test_plan.md` §2), before the reaction discussion (Q14–Q15).
- Probe: "Is second place close to first, or far behind?" / "What would change your order?"

**Q17.** "What is missing from all three statements? If you wrote the perfect one yourself, what would it say?"
- Primary tag: value proposition feedback.
- Purpose: collect gap themes for positioning refinement, in the member's language.
- Flow: after ranking and reactions, when responses have stabilized.
- Probe: "Can you say that as a single sentence?" / "Which of the three is closest to your version?"

**Q18.** "Based on your experience this year: if the platform could deliver only one thing for member companies next year, what should it be?"
- Primary tag: value proposition feedback. xref: needs (Q02).
- Purpose: free-choice priority signal to cross-check the preference test result against stated needs.
- Flow: closing question; ends the interview.
- Probe: "Why that over everything else?" / "How would you know it was working?"

## 5. Recording discipline

- Log every question with `question_id`, `answer_summary`, `probe` used, a `critical_incident` flag, and the coding fields `theme_match` / `spend_evidence` (see `results_recording_template.md`, Section B) in the per-question log.
- Verbatim quotes go to the quote log only, unchanged, marked anonymized.
- Mark `critical_incident = yes` when the interviewee describes a concrete episode with measurable cost or outcome (strongest evidence class).
